About Caicha Yum!

Caicha Yum! is a culinary micro-enterprise (UMKM) that specializes in Tongcai—a unique, flavorful blend of fermented mustard greens and napa cabbage—crafted to bring exciting new twists to traditional and modern dishes. As a micro-business, Aicha Yum! operates with low production capital, small-scale output, and a compact team, generating an annual turnover of under 300 million rupiahs, which aligns it with Indonesia’s micro-enterprise classification.

We, Caicha Yum, use only high quality vegtables whilst using authentic and traditional techniques.

Our mission is to make Tongcai memorable but also trendy!
